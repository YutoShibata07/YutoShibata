Yuto Shibata 柴田 優斗

PhD student at Keio University, currently visiting Carnegie Mellon University

I am a PhD student in the Aoki Lab at Keio University, advised by Prof. Yoshimitsu Aoki and Dr. Mariko Isogawa.

I am currently at Carnegie Mellon University with Prof. Katerina Fragkiadaki, working on physics-based humanoid control: using reinforcement learning to make simulated humanoids move the way people do.

More broadly, I work on computer vision, human motion, robotics, and sensing people with sound and wireless signals.
